How this is counted

  • Exact means the ballot marked every race the guide endorsed the way the guide did. ≤2 off and ≤4 off allow that many races to differ.
  • Fair-weather endorsers took positions in fewer than 33 contested races. They are hidden by default because a short guide is far easier to match.
  • Only races the guide endorsed and that were on that voter's ballot count. Races is the average number of the guide's races on a ballot. A guide with fewer races is easier to match.
  • A blank race counts as a miss. Ranked races use the first choice. In District 8, a first choice of Manny Yekutiel is skipped, so the next choice counts.
  • In multi-seat races, the ballot has to mark all of the guide's picks.
  • Uncontested races are left out. Election Day paper ballots are left out, because their cards can't be matched to each other.
