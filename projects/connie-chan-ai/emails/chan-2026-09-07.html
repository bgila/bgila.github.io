Archived campaign email — Labor Day message from Connie's campaign manager
Sent 2026-09-07 by Connie Chan for Congress. ← back to the analysis
 

barallies,

This is Rudy Gonzalez. I've spent my career in the labor movement — first as a Teamster at 18, then leading the San Francisco Labor Council, and now as secretary-treasurer of the SF Building & Construction Trades Council.

I didn't expect to take a leave of absence from my work at the Building Trades to run a congressional campaign. But when Connie asked, I didn't hesitate.

I want to tell you why.

I've watched Connie show up for working people in this city for years. Not just at endorsement time — at the picket line. At the budget table, fighting to protect union jobs when City Hall wanted to cut them. In the halls of power, standing up for the nurses, teachers, firefighters, hotel workers, and building trades members who actually make San Francisco run.

She's been there, again and again, when it counted.

This Labor Day, I want to say something that gets lost in the noise of a big congressional race: the most powerful thing we have isn't a TV ad or a super PAC. It's people. Organized people, showing up together, having real conversations with their neighbors. That's how labor wins. And that's how this campaign is going to win. Scott Wiener has the billionaires. He has the crypto donors and the corporate PACs. We have something better — a movement of working people who believe San Francisco shouldn't be for sale.

But movements need people to show up. This Labor Day weekend, I'm asking you to join us — knock a door, make some calls, show a neighbor why this race matters. If you can't make it out, a contribution to keep our organizers on the ground can go just as far.

This is how we win. Not by outspending them — by outworking them.

In solidarity,

Rudy Gonzalez
Campaign Manager
