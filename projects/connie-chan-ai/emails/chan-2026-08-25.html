Six days left in August, barallies.

Every month, my fundraising team sets a goal -- it's what tells us whether we can keep paying the people knocking doors across this district, keep the phones running, and keep our campaign visible in every neighborhood. That number is the difference between a field program that runs strong into November and one that has to pull back.

For this month, we're not quite to our goal yet.

I grew up in a family that had no political power and no connections. Nobody handed us anything. What we had was each other -- and the people who showed up when it counted. That's the only model of politics I trust, and the only way I know how to run a campaign.

This final week isn't about panic. It's about continuing what we said we'd do. A campaign like this doesn't run on a handful of big checks -- it runs on hundreds of people deciding, month after month, that this is worth a few dollars. That's what gets us to the number we need by the 31st.

Can you help us get there? $3 or whatever you’re able to give is exactly what we need to get across the finish line.
