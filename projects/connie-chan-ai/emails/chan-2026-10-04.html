Everything you need before San Francisco ballots go out tomorrow.
 

barallies,

Tomorrow, Monday, October 5, ballots start going out to voters across San Francisco. Before yours arrives, our team put together a quick checklist to help you get ready for the four weeks that decide this race.

  1. Make sure your ballot finds you 🗳️
    Sign up at https://california.ballottrax.net/voter/ to get a text or email when your ballot is mailed, received, and counted. If you've moved recently, you can update your registration through October 19th.

  2. Follow Connie online 📲
    Connie will be posting from the campaign trail all month, from neighborhood canvasses to community events. Follow her on Instagram and Facebook. Sharing even one of her posts puts her name in front of a friend who hasn't heard it yet.

  3. Join us on the doors (or the phones) 🚪
    Our volunteers will be out across the city as ballots land, talking with neighbors in the Richmond, the Excelsior, and everywhere in between. Weekend canvasses work great if you're local. If you're not, you can make calls from your couch. Sign up for a shift here.

  4. Show your support 🪧
    A window sign or a Connie shirt does more than you'd think, especially when your neighbors are sitting down with their ballots. Grab yours here.

  5. Chip in if you're able 💛
    Connie has never taken a dime of corporate PAC money, so this campaign runs on grassroots supporters. After a busy end of quarter, we'll keep the ask simple: if you're in a position to give $3, it helps keep our organizers talking with voters through the weeks when they're actually filling out their ballots.
