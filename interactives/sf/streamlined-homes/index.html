Streamlined Housing in San Francisco

San Francisco's housing pipeline moving through streamlined state approval laws (SB35/SB423), from preliminary filings through entitlement to construction. The map shows projects that have already opened under SB35, alongside projects currently under construction, with the latest available rendering and unit counts for each.
